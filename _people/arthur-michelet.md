Arthur Michelet is a PhD candidate in Contemporary History at the University of Lausanne (Switzerland), interested in the relation of banks and public spheres, as well as the development of multinational banks. His research focuses on collective public relations and mass-media publicity in Swiss banking (1913–1992).

In Impresso, he provides historical research and contributes to jupyter notebooks edition and web-app design.
